The problem

Stroke rarely gives a warning

It arrives fast, often in the middle of an ordinary afternoon, and the people most at risk are frequently the ones least able to catch it in time.

Sudden onset

Stroke symptoms can appear within seconds, with no prior illness to signal that something is wrong.

Signs go unnoticed

Facial drooping or arm weakness is easy to mistake for tiredness, especially when no one else is around to notice.

Aging alone

A growing share of older parents live by themselves, with no one nearby during the hours that matter most.

Can't watch 24/7

Adult children want to help, but work, distance, and daily life make constant in-person monitoring impossible.

The solution

StrokeGuard turns a simple arm test into an early signal

Built on the arm-weakness check from the BEFAST method, worn every day, and read in under a minute.

1

Raise both arms

The wearer lifts both arms shoulder-height, guided by a short prompt on the device or app.

2

Motion sensors measure movement

Onboard accelerometers compare drift, tremor, and drop speed between the left and right arm.

3

AI analysis

An on-device model compares the pattern against known signs of one-sided weakness.

4

Risk screening result

A clear result — normal, or seek medical attention — is sent to the wearer and their linked family members.

  • Continuous motion sensing

    Tracks daily arm symmetry passively, not just during a manual test.

  • One-tap arm test

    A guided 60-second check the wearer can run any time they feel off.

  • Family sync

    Results reach linked family members so no one is monitoring alone.

  • Screening, not diagnosis

    Every result nudges toward professional care rather than replacing it.
